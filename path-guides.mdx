## Jump to your path: {#-jump-to-your-path}

- **[Path A: Ready-to-Use](/path-guides/path-a-ready-to-use)** - Pens, pre-filled syringes, tablets (minimal preparation)
- **[Path B: Authentic Vial](/path-guides/path-b-authentic-vial)** - Branded medication, requires drawing doses
- **[Path C: DIY Powder](/path-guides/path-c-diy-powder)** - Highest risk, requires reconstitution (HIGH RISK)

This part provides detailed procedural information for each of the three paths described in [Getting Started](/getting-started/options-overview). Navigate to the section corresponding to your chosen path. Each path requires different steps, equipment, levels of user involvement, and carries distinct risks.

**Remember to keep the universal safety principles and dosing warnings from the [Universal Safety Principles](/universal-safety) section in mind throughout.**

## Supply List Comparison

| Supply Item | Path A<br />Ready-to-Use Pen | Path B<br />Authentic Vial | Path C<br />DIY Powder - High Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Medication Form** | Pre-filled Pen, Tablet | Manufacturer Vial | Lyophilized Powder Vial (Often "Research Use Only") |
| **Injection Syringes** | Included in Pen Device | Must Purchase Separately (Insulin Syringe, e.g., 1mL) | Must Purchase Separately (Insulin Syringe, e.g., U-100 0.3/0.5/1mL) |
| **Injection Needles** | Included | Must Purchase Separately | Must Purchase Separately (if not on syringe; SubQ gauge/length, e.g., 29-32G) |
| **Mixing Syringes** | Not Applicable | Not Applicable | Must Purchase Separately (Larger volume, e.g., 3mL or 5mL) |
| **Mixing Needles** | Not Applicable | Not Applicable | Must Purchase Separately (Larger gauge, e.g., 18-23G) |
| **Bacteriostatic Water** | Not Applicable | Not Applicable | Must Purchase Separately (Verify authenticity/sterility) |
| **Alcohol Swabs** | Must Purchase Separately | Must Purchase Separately | Must Purchase Separately |
| **Sharps Container** | Must Purchase Separately | Must Purchase Separately | Must Purchase Separately |
