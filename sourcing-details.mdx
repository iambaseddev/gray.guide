## What Stairway to Gray Actually Is

**Stairway to Gray (STG)**, full name **"STG - NO OILS"**, is a Telegram community for people who already know what they are doing with peptides. Discussion covers water-based research peptides: reconstitution, dosing protocols, stacking, vendor testing, and research papers.

**"NO OILS"** means no oil-based injectables, specifically anabolic steroids. STG does not discuss them.

This is not where you go to ask "What is bacteriostatic water?" or "How do I calculate doses?" Those questions will get ignored, or worse, you'll be removed for not doing your homework.

## STG Rules & Culture

- **Self-research expected:** Search and read before you ask.
- **Fast-paced:** High message volume. Nobody will walk you through the basics.
- **No spoon-feeding:** Generic beginner questions often go unanswered.
- **Evidence-based:** Cite sources and share data, such as test results and lab reports.
- **No oils:** Steroid and oil-based injectable discussion is off-topic.

### 3. Follow the Trail Off-Platform

1. Join the Telegram or Discord invite dropped in the comments.
2. Scroll **Pinned Messages** for vendor price lists and shipping photos.
3. Search the chat for "tracking," "customs," or "seized" to gauge delivery success.

### 4. Vet the COA Before You Buy

A Certificate of Analysis (COA) is only useful if you can tie it to the vials you actually receive. Check:

- **Purity:** The percentage of the sample that is the target peptide. Look for 98%+ from an HPLC test.
- **Content (fill amount):** The measured mg per vial. A "10mg" vial testing at 7mg is under-dosed even if purity is 99%.
- **Batch/lot number:** It must match the label on your vials. A COA for a different batch tells you nothing about yours.
- **Test date:** Recent tests matter. Old COAs reused across many batches are a red flag.
- **Lab verification:** Confirm the report on the testing lab's own website (Janoshik, for example, lets you verify by task number). Vendor-hosted PDFs can be edited.

Vendor COAs can be fake, outdated, or limited. See the [Path C risks overview](/path-guides/path-c-diy-powder) for why independent testing matters.

### 5. Pay the Way Vendors Allow

Most gray-market sellers refuse credit cards or normal PayPal checkout. A buyer can file a charge-back after delivery, and the processor claws the money back. A single charge-back can cost the vendor the sale plus a processor penalty, and can get their merchant account shut down. To avoid that fraud risk, vendors stick to **irreversible payments** like cryptocurrency.

1. **Plan to pay in crypto.** Our [Crypto Payments Guide](/sourcing-details/crypto-payments) covers Cash App, Coinbase, PayPal, and other methods step by step.
2. **Send a test transaction first.** Send a small amount and confirm the vendor received it before sending the rest.
3. **Document everything.** Screenshot the recipient address, network, and transaction ID (TXID).

### Take-Home Message

Most real leads appear in deleted Reddit comments, user profiles, or off-platform chats. Learn to:

1. **Search smart** (site filters, Reveddit).
2. **Verify the COA** against your batch and the lab's own site.
3. **Pay safely** with a test transaction first.

Follow those rules and you will weed out most scams before money changes hands.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I join Stairway to Gray?

Use the current STG Telegram invite link at the top of this page. STG rotates its invite links from time to time, and we update this page when the link changes.

### What does "NO OILS" mean?

"Oils" refers to oil-based injectables, specifically anabolic steroids. Stairway to Gray focuses on water-based research peptides and does not discuss steroids.

### Is Stairway to Gray good for beginners?

Not really. STG is a fast-paced community for experienced users and expects you to already know reconstitution, dosage math, and vendor vetting. Beginners should start with Peptide Research Group and join STG once they're comfortable.

### Can I join both STG and Peptide Research Group?

Sure. Lots of people stay in PRG even after joining STG to help new members.

### How long before I'm ready for STG?

If you're actively researching and running your own protocols, usually a few weeks to a couple of months. You'll know you're ready when basic questions start to bore you and you want deeper, scientific discussions.

### What if I join STG and feel overwhelmed?

That's normal. Drop back to Peptide Research Group, master the fundamentals, then return when you're comfortable.

### How can I find legitimate GLP-1 peptide vendors on Reddit?

Most real leads appear in deleted Reddit comments, user profiles, or off-platform chats. Use search operators like site:reddit.com with specific terms, check Reveddit for deleted content, join Telegram/Discord links from comments, and always verify vendors with test orders and third-party testing certificates.

### Should I trust vendor recommendations on Reddit?

Never trust any single source. Cross-reference vendors across multiple communities, look for consistent positive feedback over time, check for third-party testing certificates, send small test orders first, and document all transactions. Be especially wary of brand new accounts or overly enthusiastic recommendations.

### Why do gray market vendors not accept credit cards or PayPal?

A single charge-back can cost the vendor the sale plus a processor penalty and can also get their merchant account shut down. To avoid fraud risk, vendors stick to irreversible payments like cryptocurrency. This protects them from buyers who file charge-backs after receiving their order.

### What payment methods do gray market vendors typically accept?

Most vendors accept cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, USDC, USDT), which you can buy through platforms like Cash App, Coinbase, or PayPal. Cash App (US only) allows buying Bitcoin with a debit card or bank. PayPal accepts debit cards for crypto purchases. Credit cards are generally not accepted for crypto purchases.

### Is Stairway to Gray affiliated with Gray Guide?

No. They are separate communities with different focuses. STG is for advanced users; Peptide Research Group is for beginners.
