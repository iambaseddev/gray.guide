Every Mounjaro® carton (whether shipped to India, the US, or anywhere else) carries a unique 2-D barcode that you can confirm directly with Eli Lilly.

## Step-by-step check

1. **Scan the carton**  
   Open the camera on your phone or go to [scan.lilly.com](https://scan.lilly.com). Point it at the square 2-D barcode printed on the outer carton (not the vial).

2. **Read the result**  
   Lilly’s site instantly reports whether the serial number is valid, recalled, or unknown.

3. **Match carton to vial**  
   * Product name, strength, lot number, and expiry date should be identical.  
   * Flip-off cap and crimp seal must be intact.

4. **Inspect the liquid**  
   The solution inside the vial should be clear to slightly yellow, with no particles or cloudiness.

5. **When in doubt, throw it out**  
   If the scan fails, packaging looks tampered, or the liquid appears abnormal, do not use the vial. Contact Lilly customer care with the serial and lot number for guidance.

## Quick tips

* Scan every new carton before first use.  
* Keep screenshots of successful scans for your records.  
* Store vials in the fridge (2 °C – 8 °C) and discard any that have been frozen or overheated.
