* **Medication vial** - An authentic, single-dose tirzepatide vial in the strength you need. Always confirm the serial code (see [Verifying Authenticity](/path-guides/path-b-authentic-vial/authenticity)).  
* **Insulin syringes** - Sterile U-100 syringes. A 1 mL barrel with 0.01 mL markings gives the best accuracy. Use a brand-new syringe for every injection; never reuse.  
* **Needles (if separate)** - Sterile, sub-cutaneous gauge 29 G–32 G, length 5/16-inch or ½-inch, and compatible with your syringe. One new needle per shot.  
* **Alcohol swabs** - Individually wrapped, sterile pads for wiping the vial stopper and skin.  
* **Gauze or cotton** - Optional, to press on the site for a few seconds after injecting.  
* **Sharps container** - Puncture-resistant, FDA-cleared container for used syringes, needles, and empty vials.  
* **Where to buy** -  
  * Vials: through your trusted gray-market supplier (authenticate before use).  
  * Ancillary supplies: local pharmacies or reputable online medical-supply stores (see [Sourcing Ancillary Supplies](/sourcing-details/ancillary-supplies)).  
* **Disposal guidance** - Follow the [Safe Disposal Guide](/safe-disposal) for all sharps and empty vials.
