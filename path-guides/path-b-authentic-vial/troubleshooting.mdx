---

* **Cannot draw the dose**  
  * Inject the same volume of air into the vial first (see [Preparation & Use](/path-guides/path-b-authentic-vial/preparation-use)).  
  * Keep the needle tip below the liquid surface.  
  * Swap to a new needle if flow still stalls; a factory clog is rare but possible.

* **Too many air bubbles**  
  * Flick the barrel so bubbles rise to the needle hub.  
  * Push the plunger slowly to vent only the air back into the vial.  
  * Tiny “champagne” bubbles are harmless; remove large bubbles before setting the final dose line.

* **Questionable vial or liquid**  
  Do not inject if the vial is cracked, the seal is broken, or the solution looks cloudy, colored, frozen, or expired. When in doubt, discard it in a sharps container - see the [Safe Disposal Guide](/safe-disposal).
