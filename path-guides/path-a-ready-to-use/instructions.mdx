<Warning>
**Always inspect products carefully upon receipt.** Check the packaging for damage, verify the product name/dose, and check the expiry date. For injectables, visually examine the liquid before each use: it should typically be clear and colorless (Tizaro may be slightly yellow); discard if cloudy, discolored, contains particles, or if the device appears damaged or expired. Never use medication suspected of having been frozen. Follow storage instructions precisely.
</Warning>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Pen Injector" icon="pen">
    **At-a-glance**

    - **Item:** Single-use, pre-filled pen
    - **Drug inside:** Semaglutide or tirzepatide

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Choose an injection area">
        - You may inject yourself in the abdomen or the front/outer thigh.
        - A helper may inject into the back of your upper arm.
        - Each week rotate to a new spot—at least two finger-widths from the last puncture.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Remove the base cap">
        1. Make sure the lock ring shows the **locked** symbol.
        2. Grip the gray (or white) base cap and pull it straight off. Throw the cap away.
        3. Do **not** touch the hidden needle or try to replace the cap.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Place, unlock, inject">
        1. Press the clear base flat against your cleaned skin.
        2. Turn the lock ring until the unlocked symbol lines up.
        3. Push and **hold** the injection button until you hear two clicks and the internal plunger stops moving (up to 10 s).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Finish safely">
        - Lift the pen straight off the skin. The gray plunger should now fill the window.
        - Drop the entire pen into an FDA-cleared sharps container—no recapping, no re-use.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Pre-filled Syringe" icon="syringe">
    **At-a-glance**

    - **Item:** Single-dose syringe
    - **Drug inside:** Semaglutide or tirzepatide

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Prepare">
        - Wash hands. Remove one syringe from its carton by holding the barrel, not the plunger or needle cap.
        - Inspect the liquid; use only if clear and particle-free.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Clean the site">
        - Wipe the chosen abdomen, thigh, or upper-arm spot with an alcohol swab. Let it air-dry.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Uncap">
        - Hold the syringe barrel in the middle. Pull the needle cap straight off; do not twist. Discard the cap.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Pinch and insert">
        - Pinch a fold of skin.
        - Insert the needle at roughly a 45-degree angle into the fold.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Inject">
        - Relax the pinch. Push the plunger **slowly and steadily** until it stops and the barrel is empty.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Withdraw and dispose">
        - Remove the needle at the same angle.
        - Drop the syringe—needle attached—into your sharps container.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Rotate sites">
        - Choose a new area each injection to protect skin integrity and absorption.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Tablet" icon="tablets">
    **At-a-glance**

    - **Item:** Oral tablet
    - **Drug inside:** Semaglutide

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Start with an empty stomach">
        - Fast at least **8 hours** before taking the tablet.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Use plain water only">
        - Swallow the tablet whole with **no more than 120 mL** (about half a cup).
        - Do **not** split, crush, or chew.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Post-dose wait">
        - Wait **30 minutes** before eating, drinking anything other than water, or taking other oral medicines or supplements.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Daily consistency">
        - Take the tablet the same way and at roughly the same time each day for best absorption.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
