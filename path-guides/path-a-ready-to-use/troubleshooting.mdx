* **Dropped device**  
  Inspect the pen or syringe closely. If you see any crack, bend, or other damage, do **not** use it. Dispose of it in a sharps container. See the [Safe Disposal Guide](/safe-disposal).

* **Liquid looks wrong**  
  Do not inject if the solution is cloudy, oddly colored (a faint yellow tint can be normal for some products), contains particles, or may have been frozen.

* **Leak after injection**  
  A few drops on the skin usually mean the dose was not fully delivered - often because the pen was lifted too soon or the syringe plunger was not pushed to the end. Do **not** take an extra dose. Review the injection steps and wait for your next scheduled shot.

* **Device will not fire or plunger sticks**  
  If the button jams, the plunger will not move, or any part seems broken, discard the device and use a new one. Replacement or technical support is rarely available for gray-market products.
