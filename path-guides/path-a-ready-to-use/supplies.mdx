---

* **Medication** - Your ready-to-use product: pen, pre-filled syringe pack, or tablets (for example, Rybelsus®) from the gray-market vendor you choose.  
* **For injectables (pen or syringe)**  
  * **Alcohol swabs** - Buy separately to clean the skin before each injection.  
  * **Sharps container** - Buy separately for safe disposal of used pens or syringes.  
* **Where to buy ancillary items** - Alcohol swabs and sharps containers are sold at most local pharmacies or reputable online medical-supply stores. See [Sourcing Ancillary Supplies](/sourcing-details/ancillary-supplies).  
* **Disposal** - Follow the [Safe Disposal Guide](/safe-disposal) for sharps and unused medication.
