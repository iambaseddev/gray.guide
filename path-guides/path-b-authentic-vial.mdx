## Overview

Path B uses **100 percent authentic Mounjaro® tirzepatide vials manufactured by Eli Lilly for the Indian market**. Prices are lower in India because of purchasing-power parity, yet the product and its serialized packaging are identical to US stock. Authenticity can be confirmed with Lilly’s online checker (<code>scan.lilly.com</code>).

Unlike a pen, the vial is single dose. Simply draw the liquid into a sterile insulin syringe and inject - no mixing, reconstitution, or complicated math. For most beginners this is the **safest and most reliable gray-market option**.
