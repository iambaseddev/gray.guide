* **Powder will not dissolve:** Verify that you added the correct volume of BAC water during [Reconstitution](/path-guides/path-c-diy-powder/reconstitution). Continue gentle swirling or rolling; full dissolution can take several minutes. **Never shake.** If particles remain after patient mixing, assume the product is poor quality and discard it safely (see [Safe Disposal Guide](/safe-disposal)).

* **Solution is cloudy or contains particles:** **Do not inject.** Cloudiness or sediment suggests contamination, degradation, or low-quality peptide. Discard the vial in a sharps container.

* **Calculation doubts:** See the warning below.

<Danger>
**Stop.** Do not proceed if you are unsure about your [Dosage Calculation](/path-guides/path-c-diy-powder/dosage-calculation). Check the numbers with more than one reliable online calculator and seek input from experienced users (evaluate advice critically). **If any doubt remains, do not inject.** A dosing error can cause serious harm.
</Danger>
