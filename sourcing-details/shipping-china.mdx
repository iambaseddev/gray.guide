**Weight & value:** each vial of peptide (e.g. a 5 mg semaglutide vial) weighs well under 250 g and is declared at less than $800, so it clears U.S. Customs duty-free under Section 321 / Entry Type 86.  

**Transit time window:**  
  * **Best-case:** 7 days  
  * **Average:** 12 days  
  * **Maximum:** 21 days  
  
**Who touches the box:**  

  1. Factory courier in China  
  2. Export freight forwarder (YunExpress, 4PX, Yanwen, etc.)  
  3. **U.S. shipping partner / postal consolidator** (ACI Logistix, UniUni, DHL eCommerce, etc.)  
  4. Domestic carrier — usually **USPS**, but sometimes FedEx Ground Economy, UPS Mail Innovations or DHL eCommerce  
  5. Your regular mail carrier

## Step-by-step example — “Semaglutide Vial” to Austin, TX (12 days total)  

| # | Supplier checkpoint | What happens to the pen | Typical day |
|---|--------------------|-------------------------|-------------|
| 1 | Factory shipment | Courier collects sealed cartons from the GMP facility in Shenzhen. | 0 |
| 2 | Arrival at freight forwarder | Parcels are weighed and labelled at YunExpress export hub. | 0 |
| 3 | Forwarder dispatches | Thousands of small parcels (including the pen) are palletised for the daily dedicated-line flight. | 0 |
| 4 | Arrival at Hong Kong | Pallet crosses the border by truck into Hong Kong International Airport. | 0 evening |
| 5 | Export by Chinese Customs | One electronic manifest covers the whole pallet; clearance takes minutes. | 0 evening |
| 6 | Arrange flight | Space booked on an overnight HKG → LAX cargo flight. | 0 night |
| 7 | Flight departure | Aircraft lifts off just after midnight. | 1 |
| 8 | Flight arrival | Lands at LAX roughly 12 hours later. | 1 |
| 9 | Waiting for U.S. clearance | Pre-filed data lets CBP auto-release while the pallet sits in bond. | 1 |
| 10 | Start customs clearance | Automated green light under Section 321. | 1–2 |
| 11 | Clearance complete | Pallet released to ACI Logistix truck. | 2 |
| 12 | Waiting for scanning / collection | ACI unloads at its Fontana, CA hub; tracking shows **“Arrived Shipping Partner Facility – USPS Awaiting Item.”** | 2–3 |
| 13 | Transportation in U.S. | ACI prints the USPS Ground Advantage label and injects sacks at the Los Angeles SCF. | 3–4 |
| 14 | **Delivery** | USPS moves the pen across country and scans **“Out for Delivery” → “Delivered.”** | 7–12 |

*During peak weeks or major holidays, steps 12–14 can extend the journey to the 21-day maximum.*

## Carrier mix in the U.S. leg  

| Service booked in China | Stateside path | Last-mile carrier |
|------------------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Dedicated-line **Parcel** | USPS Ground Advantage label (prefix 39) | **USPS** |
| FedEx Ground Economy (ex-SmartPost) | Entirely FedEx network | **FedEx driver** |
| UPS Mail Innovations | UPS transports middle-mile, then hands to USPS (or finishes as UPS Ground Saver) | **USPS** / **UPS** |
| DHL eCommerce | DHL sort hub → USPS | **USPS** |

> **Tip:** If tracking is stuck on **“USPS Awaiting Item”** for more than five days after the first U.S. scan, open a ticket with the shipping partner named in tracking (ACI, UniUni, DHL eCommerce, etc.). They are responsible for injecting the sack into the carrier network.

## Quick recap  

* Typical delivery for a peptide vial is **12 days**, but it can be as quick as **7 days** or as long as **21 days**.  
* “Shipping Partner – USPS Awaiting Item” means USPS has the label data but not the parcel yet.  
* Watch for **“Accepted at USPS facility.”** After that, delivery is usually 2–5 days away.  
* Shipments stay under the \$800 de-minimis limit, so you **do not pay U.S. duty or tax** on arrival.

## Glossary  

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Dedicated line** | A fixed, daily charter flight reserved for e-commerce parcels. |
| **Section 321 / Entry Type 86** | U.S. rule allowing packages ≤ \$800 to clear customs duty-free with advance data. |
| **Shipping partner / postal consolidator** | U.S. warehouse (ACI Logistix, UniUni, DHL eCommerce, etc.) that receives the pallet, prints the domestic label and injects sacks into USPS/FedEx/UPS. |
| **USPS Awaiting Item** | USPS has the label information but not the physical parcel — it’s still at the partner hub. |
| **SCF / DDU** | USPS sort centers: Sectional Center Facility (regional) and Destination Delivery Unit (your local post office). |

## FAQ  

**Why does my USPS tracking show Fontana, CA when it shipped from China?**  
Fontana is ACI Logistix’s hub. They printed your U.S. label there before the pen left Shenzhen. USPS keeps showing Fontana until ACI trucks the sack over and USPS scans **“Accepted.”**

**Tracking shows “Shipping Partner – USPS Awaiting Item” for six days. Is it lost?**  
Not lost — the box is still at the partner hub. We’ll prod them; once USPS (or FedEx/UPS) scans **“Accepted,”** it usually reaches you within a week.

**Who decides whether my package rides USPS, FedEx or UPS?**  
The Chinese forwarder picks a service code based on weight, destination ZIP and cost. Two orders placed minutes apart can arrive on different carriers.

**Can I pay extra to speed it up?**  
Not right now. The dedicated-line contract keeps shipping under \$5 per parcel; faster express options would triple the cost.

**What if I order two pens?**  
As long as the combined value stays under \$800 and weight under about 450 g, they follow the same 7–21-day path. Heavier multi-item orders may ride FedEx Ground Economy for the U.S. leg.

**Do holidays matter?**  
Yes. China’s Golden Week (early October) and the U.S. Thanksgiving peak can each add 2-4 days because flights and sort hubs run at maximum load.
