Coinbase is a major US-based exchange requiring identity verification (KYC). It supports BTC, USDC, USDT, and many other assets.

## Set Up and Verify Your Account

1. Go to [coinbase.com](https://www.coinbase.com) or download the Coinbase app; click **Get started** / **Sign up**.
2. Create an account with your email and strong password.  
3. Complete identity verification: provide your name, address, date of birth, and government ID.  
4. Enable two-factor authentication (2FA).

## Link a Payment Method

1. On desktop, click your profile icon → **Settings** → **Payment methods**. On mobile, tap **Profile** → **Payment methods**.  
2. Add a **bank account (ACH)** or **debit card**. Credit cards are generally not supported for purchases.
3. Wait for verification (ACH takes 1–3 days; debit cards are instant).

## Buy Crypto (BTC, USDC, USDT, etc.)

1. On desktop, click **Buy / Sell** in the top menu. On mobile, tap **Buy** on the home screen.  
2. Select the cryptocurrency you need.  
3. Enter the amount in USD or crypto units.  
4. Choose your linked payment method.  
5. Click **Preview Buy** (desktop) or **Preview** (mobile). Review fees and total cost.  
6. Confirm by clicking **Buy now**. Crypto appears in your Coinbase wallet immediately.

## Send Crypto to the Vendor

1. On desktop, click **Send / Receive**, select **Send**. On mobile, tap **Send**.  
2. Choose the asset to send.  
3. Tap **Network** and select the correct blockchain (e.g., ERC-20 for USDC). 
4. Paste or scan the vendor’s exact wallet address. Double-check the first and last four characters.  
5. Enter the exact amount to send.  
6. Review network fees and transaction details.  
7. Click **Send now** (desktop) or **Send** (mobile).

<Warning>
Selecting the correct network is crucial for stablecoins. Sending on the wrong chain will likely result in permanent loss of funds. Always verify with your vendor.  
</Warning>
