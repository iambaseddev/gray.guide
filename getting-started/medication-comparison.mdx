## Detailed Comparisons

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Mechanism of Action">

### How Each Medication Works

**Semaglutide (GLP-1 Only)**
- Mimics the GLP-1 hormone
- Slows gastric emptying
- Reduces appetite
- Improves insulin sensitivity

**Tirzepatide (Dual Agonist)**
- Activates both GLP-1 and GIP receptors
- Enhanced glucose control
- Greater appetite suppression
- Improved metabolic effects

**Retatrutide (Triple Agonist)**
- Activates GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors
- Maximum metabolic activation
- Increased energy expenditure
- Most comprehensive appetite control
- Enhanced fat burning through glucagon activation

</Tab>

<Tab title="Efficacy & Results">

### Weight Loss Outcomes

**Semaglutide**
- Clinical trials: 15-20% body weight reduction
- Timeline: Full effects in 3-4 months
- Plateau: Common around 6-9 months

**Tirzepatide**
- Clinical trials: 20-25% body weight reduction
- Timeline: Full effects in 4-5 months
- Plateau: Less pronounced than semaglutide

**Retatrutide**
- Clinical trials: 25-30%+ body weight reduction
- Timeline: Full effects in 4-6 months
- Plateau: Still being studied
- Note: Phase 2 data shows promise for >30% weight loss in some patients

</Tab>

<Tab title="Side Effects">

### Common Side Effects by Medication

**All Three Medications Share:**
- Nausea (most common)
- Vomiting
- Diarrhea
- Constipation
- Abdominal pain
- Decreased appetite

**Severity Patterns:**
- **Semaglutide**: Generally well-tolerated after adjustment period
- **Tirzepatide**: Similar to semaglutide, possibly fewer GI issues
- **Retatrutide**: May have more initial side effects due to triple action

**Managing Side Effects:**
1. Start with lowest dose
2. Increase slowly (every 4 weeks)
3. Take with food if needed
4. Stay hydrated
5. Consider anti-nausea medication

</Tab>

## Which One Should You Choose?

### Consider Semaglutide If:
- You're new to GLP-1 medications
- You want the most established option
- You prefer maximum vendor choice
- You're sensitive to side effects

### Consider Tirzepatide If:
- You want enhanced weight loss over semaglutide
- You've plateaued on semaglutide
- You want good availability with better efficacy
- You can handle slightly more complex dosing

### Consider Retatrutide If:
- You want maximum weight loss potential
- You're comfortable with newer medications
- You can source from verified vendors
- You're willing to pay premium prices
- You're comfortable with potential for more side effects

## Important Safety Notes

<Warning>
All three medications require:
- Medical supervision
- Regular monitoring
- Proper storage and handling
- Gradual dose escalation
- Attention to side effects
</Warning>

<Info>
Retatrutide is the newest option and has less long-term safety data compared to semaglutide and tirzepatide. Consider this when making your decision.
</Info>
